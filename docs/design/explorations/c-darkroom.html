An instrument, not a diary. The home screen is a 24-hour dial: every feed and diaper is a tick around the clock, so gaps and clusters show at a glance. Insights are gauges with the expected range drawn in. Night is darkroom red on true black: red light keeps your night vision and doesn't wake anyone. Stool colors can't be judged in red, so the swatch well alone stays true-color.
